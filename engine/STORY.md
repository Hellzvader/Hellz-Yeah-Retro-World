# HELLZ YEAH RETRO WORLD — Campaign Story

Peach disappears after a massive barrel assault tears through the Mushroom Kingdom. Evidence points across the sea toward Kong territory.

Mario prepares to leave alone, but Bowser arrives furious: whoever attacked the kingdom also raided his fortress and humiliated his army. Mario and Bowser agree to a temporary alliance. Luigi joins the rescue.

Their journey crosses broken Mushroom Kingdom roads, jungle borders, treetop settlements, mines, ships, factories and the Kong stronghold. Mushroom Kingdom enemies and Kong-country enemies appear throughout the campaign as the conflict spreads.

## Playable launch team
- Mario — balanced hero; fire-based power ability.
- Luigi — higher jump and aerial control.
- Bowser — high health, fire breath and smash strength.

## Campaign goal
Reach the Kong stronghold, discover why Peach was taken, defeat the forces guarding her and rescue her.

## Asset rule
The project implements the fan-game characters, mechanics and encounters in code, but does not copy or bundle Nintendo ROM data, ripped sprites, music or sound effects. Art in the repository is original placeholder art until replacement assets are supplied.
