# Campaign implementation map

## World 1 — Broken Kingdom
Mario and Luigi discover the barrel raid. Bowser arrives after his fortress was hit too. The alliance begins.
Boss gate: Kong Guardian.

## World 2 — Jungle Canopy
Vines, barrels, treetop platforms, flying enemies and vertical routes.
Boss gate: Kong Guardian.

## World 3 — Armada
Ships, moving hazards and storm sections.
Boss gate: Kong Guardian.

## World 4 — Factory
Machines, heat hazards and conveyor-style encounters.
Boss gate: Kong Guardian.

## World 5 — Kong Stronghold
Fortress approach, Peach's prison and the final rescue encounter.

## Engine gameplay systems now represented
- Mario / Luigi / Bowser switching
- individual movement/HP/abilities
- projectile combat
- Bowser smash
- stomping
- enemy HP and AI
- bosses gating exits
- stage progression
- power-up definitions
- TikFinity queue parser and viewer-tagged enemy spawning
- editor/runtime level format

Next implementation targets: animated sprite system, richer enemy state machines, barrels that can be carried/thrown, moving platforms, swimming/climbing, checkpoints, dialogue/cutscenes, save slots, audio mixer, controller remapping, editor GUI, and Windows packaging.
