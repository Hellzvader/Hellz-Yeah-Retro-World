# Controls
## Play
A/D or arrows: move
Space/Z: jump
Q/E: switch Mario, Luigi, Bowser
X/J: attack
C/K: special (Bowser smash)
W/S or up/down: climb vines / swim
V/L: interact / barrel action
R: restart stage
F5: editor/play-test toggle

## Editor
1 ground, 2 platform, 3 enemy, 4 collectible, 5 barrel, 6 vine, 7 exit
Left click places an object
A/D or arrows pan
F2 saves the current .hyrworld level
F5 play-tests it
